# Careers in Cyber (TryHackMe)

Link: https://tryhackme.com/room/careersincybersn

What it was: Not a tech lab, but an overview of career opportunities in cybersecurity — what roles exist, how much they pay, and why you don't need a computer science degree to get started.

What I learned:
1. Millions of vacancies remain unfilled in cybersecurity — demand is much higher than the supply of specialists
2. You don’t need a specialized diploma or years of study — certificates and practice can lead to a good paid job faster than in medicine or law
3. There are over 50 different roles in cybersecurity (pentesters, SOC analysts, incident responders, etc.) — each one suits a different type of thinking
4. I took an interactive quiz that helps determine which role in cybersecurity is right for me

What I learned: Cybersecurity is not one profession, but an entire industry with different directions, and my background or age is not an obstacle to entry — the main thing is to practice consistently.
